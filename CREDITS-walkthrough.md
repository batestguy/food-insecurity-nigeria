# Walkthrough video — what it is and what is in it

`ConferenceMaths-walkthrough-olufunmi.mp4` — two-minute captioned tour of the
manuscript, built from the submitted PDF (`paper.pdf`) rather than from
screenshots, so every page shown is the page in the paper.

- Cover: the journal's call-for-papers poster.
- Body: abstract, the Introduction's Nigerian literature paragraph, the model,
  Propositions 1, 4 and 11 with their proofs, Table 1, the calibration table,
  Figures 1, 2 and 4, the Conclusions and the reference list.
- Closing: a card listing what the paper set out to do and what it delivered.

Rebuilt with `build_walk.py` (slides) and ffmpeg (assembly); the silent master
`ConferenceMaths-walkthrough.mp4` carries a synthesised tone bed instead of the
music.

## Music

The audio is **Styl-Plus, "Olufunmi"** (2003). It is included in this video as a
personal demonstration, without a licence from the rights holders, and is not
part of the manuscript, the submitted paper or any published result. The track
is not redistributed as an audio file — only as the soundtrack of this demo.

If a rights holder objects, delete this file and its history entry; the silent
version is unaffected and is the one to keep.
